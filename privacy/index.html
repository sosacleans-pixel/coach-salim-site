Privacy notice

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as which pages are visited, how long visits last and the general type of device and browser. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. We also record anonymous events when you click a link to Amazon or submit the contact form (for example, which link was clicked, or that the form was sent); these events never include your name, email or message. We use this information only to understand and improve the site. We do not sell it. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Contact form

When you send a message through the contact form, the name, email address, phone number, selected service and message you enter are emailed to Coach Salim so he can reply to you. The form is delivered by the email-forwarding service FormSubmit. Your details are used only to respond to your inquiry.

Questions
