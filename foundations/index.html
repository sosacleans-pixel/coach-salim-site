A free 4-week program to build strength, mobility, balance and stamina for everyday life and the outdoors. Week 1 is bodyweight only. Each week adds a little more weight, until Week 4 has full weighted sessions with a dumbbell or kettlebell.

What you build

Strong, steady and ready to move.

Functional means useful. The workouts train the patterns you use on stairs, hills, trails, bikes and long days on your feet. They suit beginners, people coming back after a break, and hikers, runners and riders who want a stronger base.

Strength

Squat, hinge, lunge, push, pull and carry, taught from the ground up.

Mobility

Ankles, hips and upper back get attention in every warm-up and cooldown.

Balance

Single-leg work that builds steady footing on uneven ground.

Single-leg stability

Step-ups, reverse lunges and single-leg reaches for stronger, more even legs.

Core

Dead bugs, planks and carries to keep your trunk strong and steady.

Work capacity

Short, simple circuits and easy cardio days to build stamina at your own pace.

The 4 weeks

Bodyweight first. Weights when you are ready.

No equipment is needed to start. You add a light dumbbell or kettlebell in Week 2, and build to full weighted sessions by Week 4. Every move comes with an easier option, so you can stay at bodyweight for as long as you like.

Week 1 · Bodyweight only

Bodyweight foundations

Learn the movement patterns and build the habit. Everything this week is bodyweight.

Equipment this weekNo weights needed
  • A sturdy chair
  • A stair or low step
  • A wall or counter
  • A mat or towel (optional)
Week 2 · Light weight

Bodyweight plus a light weight

Keep the same patterns and meet your first light weight on a few moves.

Equipment this weekOne light dumbbell or kettlebell (optional)
  • One light dumbbell or kettlebell, or a backpack with a few books or water bottles
  • A sturdy chair
  • A stair or low step
  • A mat or towel (optional)
Week 3 · Moderate weight

Mostly weighted

Most main moves now use a weight. Bodyweight versions stay available for every move.

Equipment this weekOne moderate dumbbell or kettlebell (a pair is a bonus)
  • One moderate dumbbell or kettlebell, or a loaded backpack or a water jug
  • A sturdy chair or bench
  • A stair or step
  • A mat or towel (optional)
Week 4 · Challenging weight

Full weighted sessions

Put it all together with full weighted sessions, then re-do your self-check from Week 1.

Equipment this weekOne or two dumbbells or kettlebells, challenging but controlled
  • One or two dumbbells or kettlebells (or a heavy loaded backpack)
  • A sturdy chair or bench
  • A stair or step
  • A mat or towel (optional)

No weights at home? A backpack with a few books or water bottles, or a filled water jug, works as a weight for most moves. Every exercise lists a no-weights option.

Your week

Three strength days. One easy day if you want it.

Each strength session takes about 40 minutes and includes a warm-up, the main work, and a cooldown. Space them out with a rest or walking day between, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

Day 1Legs and balance
Day 2Push, pull and carry
Day 3Full body and work capacity
OptionalEasy cardio and mobility: a walk, hike, bike or easy jog, plus a mobility flow

Every workout shows the warm-up, the exercises with sets, reps and rest, form cues, an easier option and a harder option. In Weeks 1 and 4 you also do a short 5-minute self-check, so you can see your own progress.

Safety first. This program is general fitness information for healthy adults and is not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have a health condition, an injury, or you are pregnant. Stop and rest if you feel sharp pain, dizziness or chest discomfort. Choose weights and ranges that feel controlled and comfortable.
