About Coach Salim

It doesn't get easier. We get stronger.

Salim was never the athletic type. In his mid-30s, working through some hard personal seasons, he saw how movement, nutrition and mindset are all connected, and it changed the direction of his life.

Before coaching, Salim was a professional civil engineer in the Dominican Republic, working on government infrastructure and socio-economic projects, including helping create a national situation room and disaster-response team. He went on to run businesses in real estate, digital marketing and hospitality. Then a 7-minute daily routine, done every day for eight months, changed his energy, his focus and eventually his career.

Today he runs Coach Salim Heyaime LLC from Salt Lake County, Utah, coaching clients in person and online. His approach blends fitness, nutrition, yoga, biomechanics, mindfulness and accountability, for people just starting out and for people optimizing performance.

“Ultimately, my brand is about movement with meaning. It is about helping people build stronger bodies, calmer minds, and a more intentional way of living.”Salim Heyaime, in Voyage Utah
